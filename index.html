// MY APPROACH01 — 03 / DISCOVER · DESIGN · REFINE

Curiosity first.
Clarity by design.

I love untangling complex problems. I ask questions, break challenges into smaller pieces, and explore what people actually need before deciding what to design.

Research gives my curiosity direction. I turn what I learn into clear, visual, practical experiences — then test and refine them until they make sense in real life.

01 / DISCOVER + DEFINEFind the problem beneath the problem.Ask why, understand people, spot patterns, and challenge assumptions through research.
02 / EXPLORE + DESIGNMake the complicated feel simple.Experiment with flows, digital wireframes, and interactive prototypes. Balance usability with visual craft.
03 / TEST + REFINELet real interactions guide the next step.Observe what works, learn from feedback, and improve with purpose — not just polish.

// RESEARCH

Understand people.
Design with purpose.

I pursued HCI because I wanted to go beyond designing interfaces that look good. I wanted to understand how people think, what they struggle with, and why certain experiences work.

I love finding patterns in what people say and do, then using those insights to question assumptions and shape practical design decisions.

01 / LISTEN + EXPLOREStart with people, not pixels.Use interviews, surveys, and competitive analysis to understand needs, motivations, and context.
02 / FIND + EVALUATEConnect the dots.Synthesize patterns and uncover friction through usability testing, heuristic evaluation, and walkthroughs.
03 / APPLY + IMPROVEMake insights useful.Translate findings into clearer flows, thoughtful interactions, and prototypes that can be tested and refined.

// ABOUT ME

Hey, I’m Rajatha! 👋

I’m a UI/UX Designer passionate about creating seamless digital experiences through thoughtful design and empathy-driven decisions. I enjoy turning complex ideas into intuitive user journeys and meaningful product experiences.

I believe in one simple principle: “I am not my user.” It reminds me to look beyond my own assumptions, let research guide my decisions, and design around what people actually need.

So, how did I get here?

My journey started as a Statistics and Computer Science student with a strong interest in art and creativity. I began exploring design by creating posters and working on web design projects during college. What started as a creative interest soon sparked my curiosity about UI/UX, and I haven’t looked back since. I loved discovering how design, technology, and human needs come together to shape the products we use every day.

I later worked as a UI/UX Designer, but I wanted to go beyond creating visually appealing interfaces. I wanted to understand the people behind the screens. That curiosity led me to pursue my Master’s in Human-Computer Interaction at DePaul University, where I developed a deeper understanding of user research, usability, and interaction design.

Today, I’m excited to bring together my design skills, prototyping experience, and research-driven perspective to help teams create products that are both useful and enjoyable.

Outside of design, I’m naturally curious, love experimenting with ideas, and have a soft spot for solving puzzles. When I’m not designing, you’ll probably find me gaming, going for a bike ride, or exploring quiet, lesser-known places.

I guess I’m just someone who enjoys discovering new things and figuring them out along the way.
